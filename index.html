ESP32 Pinout Wiring Guide

Reference schematics for connecting physical sensors, motor testbed, BMS, buzzer, and LEDs to the ESP32

GPIO 4
DHT11 Data Pin Requires 4.7kΩ – 10kΩ pull-up resistor to 3.3V
GPIO 21 & 22
MPU6050 I2C Bus (SDA / SCL) Hardware I2C fast-mode at 400kHz for vibration RMS & tilt angles
GPIO 34
Rain Sensor Analog Out (AOUT) Connected to ADC1 Channel 6 for rain intensity measurement
GPIO 23
Rain Sensor Digital Out (DOUT) LM393 comparator output for fast interrupt rain detection
GPIO 35
3.7V LiPo Battery Sense (ADC) Resistor divider (100kΩ / 100kΩ) connected to ADC1 Channel 7
GPIO 18
Testbed Motor PWM Driver Gate signal to logic-level N-MOSFET for motor speed & vibration induction
GPIO 19
Active Piezo Buzzer Pin Direct logic output driving 85dB high-frequency annunciator tone
GPIO 2
Alarm Indicator LED Current-limited visual warning indicator (active HIGH)
VIN (5V) & Solar
TP4056 BMS & Motor Power Rail Solar harvesting input and regulated 5V rail for motor testbed
3.3V & GND
Sensor VCC & Common Ground Regulated 3.3V logic supply and unified ground plane
